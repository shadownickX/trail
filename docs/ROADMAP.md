# TRAIL roadmap

## Phase 1 — Trace the rotation

- contract-address input;
- configurable seller lookback;
- recognized-sale classification;
- next qualifying purchase per seller;
- grouped destination-token table;
- timestamps, amounts, and continuation state.

**Exit criterion:** a user can reproduce every displayed route from its referenced transactions.

## Phase 2 — Open the destination

- deployer identity by address;
- previous observed launches;
- available funding provenance;
- fee recipient and creator tax;
- deployer and linked-cohort concentration.

**Exit criterion:** every relationship has a literal label and a source reference.

## Phase 3 — Live investigation

- incremental updates;
- hold / add / exit changes;
- material-change subscriptions;
- result caching and pre-indexed history;
- coverage and provider-health indicators.

**Exit criterion:** update latency and completeness are measured and published.

## Phase 4 — Research workspace

- saved investigations;
- watchlists;
- comparable destination-token views;
- JSON and Markdown evidence export;
- user-defined relationship filters.

**Exit criterion:** a saved investigation can be reviewed without trusting an unexplained score.

## Not planned

TRAIL does not need private-key import, transaction signing, or an automatic `SAFE` label to deliver its core value.
